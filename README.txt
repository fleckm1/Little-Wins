Little Wins PWA

Files:
- index.html
- manifest.webmanifest
- service-worker.js
- icon-192.png
- icon-512.png

To install on iPhone, these files must be hosted on a website served over HTTPS.
Once hosted:
1. Open the site in Safari.
2. Tap Share.
3. Tap Add to Home Screen.
4. Tap Add.

The app will then launch in its own standalone window and can work offline after the first load.
